# Exodus: the runner

A single-player endless runner (three lanes; jump and double jump, change lanes, duck once the run has gone on a minute, run along platforms, dodge flying quail) through the Exodus story, with Bible questions answered by running through one of three gates. Status: **playable** in the Adventures tab (`exodus` screen); `/exodus-lab` (dev only) is the test page with autopilot and switches.

## Decisions so far

- **Setting:** fleeing Egypt with Pharaoh's chariots behind. The places follow one another along the run: Egypt's brick yards and town → a beach → the Red Sea (walking between the walls of water, Exodus 14:22) → later the wilderness, the Jordan, Jericho.
- **Questions never interrupt dodging.** One cycle:
  1. **Run** 10–30 s (varies) with obstacles.
  2. **Warn** ~1 s: a horn, the track ahead clears (obstacles sink into the sand), the world starts slowing.
  3. **Read**: slow motion (×0.14), the world goes soft (a blur on the canvas and a dark veil), the question on a scroll. Reading time fits the text: 3 s + 0.22 s a word, 4–8 s. **Ready** skips the rest.
  4. **Speed up** ~1.6 s back to full speed. The question stays as a one-line strip at the top, and **the three answers appear over their lanes** (the one over your lane is lit), so there are **5.6 s to choose a lane** before the gates.
  5. **Gates**: three gates (one per lane) with the same answers, reached 4 s after the speed-up. The right gate: gold burst, multiplier +1. **The wrong gate ends the run.** The run-over card shows the question, the right answer and the reference (learning moment; flag button to come).
  6. **Breather** ~1.6 s with no obstacles, then back to the run.
- Gate questions come from the bank: difficulty ≤ 3, text ≤ 120 characters, every answer ≤ 22 characters (they must fit a sign at speed); one wrong answer of the four is dropped at random, the three left are shuffled into the lanes.
- **Obstacles:** a jump (brick pallet / reef), a block to go round (straw cart, statue sledge / boulder) and, **from about one minute of running** (slow motion not counted), something to **duck under** (linen on a beam / coral arch, underside at 1.5; swipe down to roll, or in the air to drop and roll on landing). The first one comes right across all three lanes, alone in its row, with a one-time "Swipe down to duck!" hint; later duck rows sometimes have a block in one lane. Every row has a way through.
- **Double jump:** swipe up again in the air (or tap) for a second, smaller jump with a forward somersault (`JUMP2_V`); one per jump, and one after running off an edge. High enough to clear a block (`OVER_BLOCK_Y` 3.1) or a duck obstacle (`OVER_DUCK_Y` 3) with good timing.
- **Platforms** (from ~25 s, `PLATFORMS_FROM_S`): a long thing to run along in one or two lanes, 14 long with the top at 1.5 (`PLATFORM`): Egypt and Jericho a dressed stone on log rollers, the sea a rock shelf with coral, the wilderness a sandstone ledge, the Jordan a grey one. Half have a ramp up their near end (run up it); the others must be jumped onto (running into the front is head-on, like a block). Lights run along the top. The ground under the runner is `floorAt` (the highest platform under them, rising along a ramp); off the end or the side the runner falls. The row reports its length to `spawn`, so the next row comes after it. A question sinks only what starts more than 6 ahead, never the platform underfoot.
- **Quail** (from ~40 s, `FLOCKS_FROM_S`; Numbers 11:31, "about two cubits above the ground"): a flock a lane wide flies at the runner (`FLY_SPEED` 9 once within `FLY_FROM` 45, placed further on so it meets the runner about where its row is): low over one or two lanes (jump, or go round), or after the first minute at head height right across (duck). Two wing poses (`quailA`/`quailB`), flipped by the scene; a cleared flock flies up instead of sinking.
- **Feedback:** whatever is earned flies from the runner into its box in the top bar (lights into XP, each coin into the coins), and the number counts up as it lands. Glancing a block from the side or failing a jump/roll is a **stumble** (speed drops); a second stumble within 6 s, or running head-on into a block, means the chariots catch you.
- **Rewards** (`EXODUS_REWARDS` in `shared/src/coins.ts`), mostly XP so the runner can't be farmed for coins: lights along the track 10 XP each; a shekel (1 coin) only every 10–15 s of running; a right gate 500 XP + 3 coins; at most 60 coins a run (about the best Bible Blitz game). Paid when the run ends, like Bible Blitz (one grant, inside the server's daily caps). Simulated perfect play: ~2,100 XP and 6–8 coins a minute; a perfect Bible Blitz pays ~10–12 coins and up to ~3,000 XP a minute.
- **Server load:** none while running. The run lives entirely on the device; the server sees one reward grant at the end, and static files (the model is 113 KB with Brotli, 135 KB gzipped; three.js is the chunk the 3D city already uses).
- **Score** = distance × multiplier; the multiplier grows with every right gate. Speed rises from 15 to 30 units/s.
- **Navigation:** the Adventures tab (single-player games, Exodus first) sits between Board games and Jerusalem; the Leaderboard has no tab any more: the 🏆 next to your portrait in the top bar opens it.
- **The screen:** a start card (your best, ⓘ How to play; the intro shows once before the first run), a pause button (the run also pauses itself when the app goes to the background; End the run pays like any other end), and the end card: why it ended, for a wrong gate the question, the right answer, the verse in your language and a flag button; distance, gates, XP and coins earned, new best. Pharaoh's chariots (a pair, plumed horses, blue-crowned drivers) close in behind you after a stumble and fall back after 6 s of clean running; they catch you on the second stumble or a head-on crash.
- **Look:** 3D, but not plain: cel shading (`MeshToonMaterial` with a 4-band ramp), a curved world (the track rolls away over a hill, done in the vertex shader), warm sunset sky, strong sun with shadows near the runner, chunky props in a few clear shapes, a dark outline round the runner, foam on the walls of water, dust, sparks, drifting sand or bubbles, speed lines at the screen's edges when the run is fast.

## Code

- `app/src/exodus/sim.ts`: the run without drawing: tiles, things placed by track distance `s` (drawn at z = dist − s, the runner stays at z = 0), the runner (lane, jump, stumble), obstacle rows (always one way through), lights and shekels, the run's XP and coins, the question cycle and phases, `autopilot` for the lab.
- `app/src/exodus/scene/`: `ExodusScene.tsx` (the `ExodusWorld` class: sky, tile pool, every model instanced by kind, water walls, coins, gates, lights, camera, effects; the component only attaches it and steps it in `useFrame`), `props.ts` (all models built with the city's `Geo`), `curve.ts` (curved-world shader patch, toon ramp, sky colours), `sea.ts` (the walls of water shader), `fx.ts` (particles), `textures.ts` (road, sand, sea floor, gate signs painted at load), `runner.ts` (the character).
- `app/src/exodus/controller.ts` (`RunController`): the run on screen, shared by the game and the lab: question deck (`questions.ts`: difficulty ≤ 3, short texts and answers, flagged ones excluded) and when to ask (every 10–30 s of running), swipes and keys, pause, the HUD written every frame, flyers into the top bar, `takeResult` once per finished run. `ExodusRun.tsx`: the canvas, top bar, scroll, answers over the lanes, hints. `best.ts`: the best distance on this device.
- `app/src/screens/AdventuresScreen.tsx` (the tab) and `app/src/screens/exodus/ExodusScreen.tsx` (the game: start, pause, end card, paying).
- `app/src/exodus/ExodusLab.tsx`: the dev page (swipe or arrow keys; panel: switch place, question now, autopilot, wrong gate, no crashes). `window.__exodus` (the controller) in dev, also on the game screen.
- The runner is KayKit's "Rogue_Hooded" (Kay Lousberg, **CC0**; `app/public/exodus/LICENSE-kaykit.txt`), stripped to the 9 animations used (run, jump start/air/land, hit, death, cheer, idle) with gltf-transform and then made smaller (see Performance below): `runner.glb`, 422 KB (113 KB with Brotli). The cloak's green swatches are repainted at load (`repaint`), so the player's look colours can be applied later. `Runner` reshapes the chibi so it reads as a runner from behind: narrower body (`SLIM_X`/`SLIM_Z`), smaller head (`HEAD_SCALE`), legs 18 % longer with the hips raised to match, the cape shortened so the legs show, and the run clip's legs and arms swung `STRIDE` × further round the clip's average pose (by the run clip's weight). On top, a squash-and-stretch spring: stretch on take-off, squash on landing, down to `DUCK_HEIGHT` while ducking (plus a spray of sand), and a slight forward lean. The mixer only writes a bone when the clip's value changes, so `step` puts back the clip's own values before each update; otherwise the changes would compound.
- Texts: `exodus` i18n namespace.

## Added after the first playable version

- **The route** (`ROUTE` / `routeAt` in `sim.ts`): Egypt 700 → (a beach) → the Red Sea 640 → the wilderness 900 → the Jordan 340 → Jericho 800, then round again from the wilderness. The wilderness: red rock, acacias, the camp's goat-hair tents and fires, now and then the Tabernacle in its court or the bronze serpent (Numbers 21:9), Mount Sinai under its cloud on the horizon. The Jordan: a pebbly riverbed, the water heaped up on the left (Joshua 3:16), reeds and willows, the ark with its priests or the twelve memorial stones. Jericho: its mud-brick wall with towers and houses on the right, now and then Rahab's scarlet cord (Joshua 2:18), palms on the left. Each place has its own obstacles (`OBSTACLES`) and haze.
- **Power-ups**, one every 25–40 s of running from ~20 s on, leading a line of lights: a jar of manna (10 s: every light near you, in any lane, is gathered), the pillar of cloud (up to 20 s: the next stumble or crash is forgiven), Moses' staff (at once: everything in sight ahead sinks away). A banner says what it does; manna and cloud show as pills with a running-down bar.
- **"66" gates** (`books.ts`): one question in four asks which book comes after (or before) another, with two books near it as the wrong answers; book names as each language's Bibles name them. No verse or flag on those.
- **Daily Run**: the start card's second button. Seeded by the UTC date (`daySeed`), so the track, the questions and when they come are the same for everyone that day (as far as stumbles let the same rows come at the same place). Best today on this device (`sixtysix.exodusDaily.v1`).
- **Online leaderboard** (`server/src/exodus.ts`, `shared/src/exodus.ts`, `app/src/exodus/scoresApi.ts`): every finished run of a signed-in player is sent (`POST /api/exodus/run { distance, seconds, daily }`, checked for plausibility: distance ≤ seconds × 31 + 60, at most 60 runs an hour); the table `exodus_runs` keeps each player's best all time, per week and per Daily Run day (week/day rows pruned after 60 days; in the delete-account transaction). `GET /api/exodus/leaderboard?board=all|week|daily` (guests too): top 20 and your rank. The Leaderboard screen switches between XP and Exodus (this week / all time / Daily Run).
- **Your look**: the cloak and trim per character (`scene/looks.ts`), the hands and face in your skin tone.
- **Sounds**: jump, double jump, land, lane, roll, a light chime climbing a scale for lights in a row, shekel, power-up, the cloud, the staff, hooves when the chariots come up, a fanfare through the right gate (`run*` in `game/sfx.ts`).
- **Slow phones**: over each 2 s of running below ~40 fps the quality steps down for the rest of the run: resolution 1, then no shadows, then resolution 0.75 (`watchSpeed`).

## Performance

Measured in desktop Chrome at 430 × 860 (the numbers a phone gets are still to be measured). Keep these properties when changing the scene.

- **Frames on demand.** The canvas is `frameloop="demand"` and `ExodusScene` asks for each next frame itself: every frame while the run is on; 30 a second while the question is read (`READ_FPS`: slow motion behind the blur and the scroll); 20 a second behind the end card once the fall has played (`OVER_FPS` after `OVER_FULL_S` 4 s: sand, chariots and water still move); one a second while paused (`PAUSED_FPS`: the start and pause cards, where nothing moves, so it only repairs a lost picture). `RunController.setPaused` / `restart` / `quit` wake the scene at once (`onWake`). The frame after a wait is not counted as a slow frame (`real` is capped at 0.1 s).
- **The blur while reading** is CSS (`.is-warned` / `.is-reading` on `.ex-root`, `filter: blur(3px)` on the canvas, `exodus.css`): the drawing buffer is never resized (it used to be redrawn at 0.3 of the resolution, a resize at each end of every question). A phone blurs the full screen on every frame it is given, hence the 30 frames a second while it lasts.
- **Nothing allocated per frame** in `ExodusWorld.frame`, `Run.step`, `Runner.update`, `Particles` and `RunController.frame`: particles live in a fixed pool and are emitted as `emit(PUFF.kind, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz)` (the kinds are made once); the sim's lists close up in place; `takeEvents` hands out one of two arrays in turn (good until the next call); the HUD is written only when a number changes. What is left is the engine's own (numbers boxed in calls, three's sort and uniforms): about 4.9 → 2.1 MB/s by `performance.memory` over 20 s of running.
- **One material per kind of mesh.** three works a material's shader out again whenever the same material moves between an instanced, a plain and a skinned mesh. So the instanced models share `propMat`, the gates and chariots have `plainMat`, and in the shadow pass the plain meshes (`plainDepth`) and the runner (skinned body, plain cape) have `customDepthMaterial`s of their own. Before, this cost about 0.8 MB/s of garbage and two shader look-ups a frame.
- **What is not drawn.** `frustumCulled` stays off for the instanced models, the walls of water, the sky and the particles (the shader bends the world, and one model's instances line the whole track), but: a model with nothing out is hidden; **under the horizon** nothing is drawn: the track drops by `bend.down` × ahead², so from the camera the ground goes out of sight at a crest about 58 ahead, and a thing `top` high `d` beyond it is hidden once `bend.down` × d² > top (`hidden`, `place`; ground tiles, walls of water, models and pickups); and the **shadow pass** draws only the instances that can throw a shadow into the sun's shadow map (`SHADOW_AHEAD`, `onBeforeShadow`). Egypt: 93 → 71 draw calls, 138,000 → 69,000 triangles a frame; the sea 110,000 → 51,000. Check after changing the camera, the bend or the shadow camera: paused, a frame with `window.__exodusWorld.cull = false` must be the same picture as with `true` (30 pairs over the five places were identical).
- **Nothing compiled or uploaded mid-run.** When the scene is attached and again when the runner has loaded, `compileAsync` compiles every shader in the scene, shown or not; then one frame nobody sees (`warmUp`, drawn over at once) has every model, every place's ground and the gates and chariots in it, which sends their buffers, textures and the shadow pass's shaders to the graphics card. The signs carry an empty picture from the start. The number of programs (`?stats=1`) is 15 behind the start card and stays 15 through every place, question, power-up and stumble (it used to grow from 13 to 16 during the run). Not covered: `watchSpeed` turning the shadows off on a slow phone compiles the lit shaders again, once.
- **The model.** `runner.glb` made smaller without a decoder at run time: animation keys resampled with a looser tolerance, normals as bytes, texture coordinates and weights as short integers (`KHR_mesh_quantization`, which three's loader reads itself). Positions stay floats: quantizing them splits the skin in six and puts a scale on the cape's node, which `Runner` sets. 506 → 422 KB, with Brotli 148.9 → 112.6 KB, gzipped 181 → 135 KB. Meshopt was tried and is worse here once Brotli is applied (133 KB, plus a 29 KB decoder). From the original file (gltf-transform 4.5.1; the last line declares the extension, which the tool leaves out when positions are skipped, so the file validates):
  ```bash
  npx @gltf-transform/cli@4.5.1 resample runner.glb step1.glb --tolerance 0.001
  npx @gltf-transform/cli@4.5.1 quantize step1.glb runner.glb --pattern '!POSITION' --quantize-normal 8
  node -e 'const fs=require("fs"),f="runner.glb",b=fs.readFileSync(f),n=b.readUInt32LE(12),j=JSON.parse(b.subarray(20,20+n));j.extensionsUsed=j.extensionsRequired=["KHR_mesh_quantization"];let s=Buffer.from(JSON.stringify(j));s=Buffer.concat([s,Buffer.alloc((4-s.length%4)%4,32)]);const h=Buffer.from(b.subarray(0,20));h.writeUInt32LE(s.length,12);h.writeUInt32LE(b.length-n+s.length,8);fs.writeFileSync(f,Buffer.concat([h,s,b.subarray(20+n)]))'
  npx @gltf-transform/cli@4.5.1 validate runner.glb
  ```
  The model's 1024 px palette picture is used at 256 px (`PALETTE_PX`): a sixteenth of the texture memory, and the repaint in your look's colours is 16× quicker.
- **The look's extras** (graphics plan, Phase 4 item 3), all without a post-processing pass:
  - *The runner's outline*: every skinned part drawn once more inside out, each vertex pushed 0.03 out along its normal before the skinning (`outlineMaterial` in `runner.ts`), in one dark colour. Six small draw calls and about 4,500 triangles (the runner is six skinned parts), one more shader (16 programs, compiled with the rest, still constant through a run). The cape has none: a flat sheet has no hull. The camera keeps its distance, so the line keeps its width (a pixel or two).
  - *Speed lines*: eight pale streaks at the screen's edges, DOM elements moved by CSS (`.ex-speed`, `STREAKS` in `ExodusRun.tsx`; transform and opacity only), pointing away from where the track runs off to. On while the world speeds up after a question and from run speed 24 (`FAST_SPEED`; the run goes 15 → 30); the controller sets `.on` only when that changes. Off and standing still while paused, over or reading; `display: none` under reduced motion.
  - *Foam on the walls of water*: in the walls' own shader (`sea.ts`), no new draw call: the bands at the crest and the foot have a broken fringe of drifting flecks (three sines) and a bright line where the wall meets the ground; foam is opaque.
  - On the lowest quality step (`watchSpeed`, resolution 0.75) the outline and the speed lines are dropped (`ExodusWorld.lean`).
  - Cost in desktop Chrome: Egypt 69 → 75 draw calls, 67,300 → 72,100 triangles; frame time and the script's share of it unchanged (16.7 ms, about 1 ms).
- Dev: `window.__exodusWorld` (the scene), `window.__exodusGl` (the renderer), `window.__exodus` (the controller).
