# Review notes: prophets (batches 180–189)

523 new questions (Isaiah through Malachi, Lamentations excluded). All 523 pass verify.mjs; none are in review-queue or rejected.
German and Hungarian answers follow the Bible text the app shows (Schlachter 1951, Károli 1908) where it differs from the WEB. Because of that, the three languages sometimes name a different thing for the same fact. Those cases are listed below.

## Content and distractors
- q_b181_010 (Isa 37:38): the distractors Adrammelech and Sharezer are named in the same verse as the sons who killed Sennacherib. Per the text they are clearly wrong, but the question rewards close reading (d5).
- q_b183_034 (Jer 40:13-14): Johanan *and the captains* warned Gedaliah. The answer names Johanan only.
- q_b186_056 (Dan 10:13): the distractors Raphael and Uriel are not angel names in the Protestant canon. Swap them if that matters.
- q_b188_052 (Zech 6:2): red (first chariot) against black, white and dappled (the other chariots in 6:2-3). This is a pure detail question.
- q_b189_002 (Jonah 1:5, d1): the distractors Paul and Peter are deliberately easy.
- q_b184_002 (Ezek 1:2): the answer reads "The fifth" (year). 1:2 also has "the fifth of the month", but the question asks for the year.
- q_b185_036 (Ezek 48:35): WEB says "18,000 reeds" but Károli says "sing" (cubits), so DE/HU leave out the unit.
- Difficulty mix ended up at d1 29 / d2 117 / d3 191 / d4 134 / d5 52. That is light on d1, because most famous prophet facts (Jonah's fish, Daniel's lions, Isa 53, etc.) are already in the bank. The labels are my judgement.
