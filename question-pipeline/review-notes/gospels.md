## Textual / translation-base doubts
- q_b202_048 (Mark 9:29 "prayer and fasting"): "and fasting" is missing in Luther 2017 and most modern Hungarian Bibles. Schlachter has it, but the Biblica HU verse shown may not.
- q_b202_087, q_b202_088 (Mark 16:9, 16:19): from the longer ending of Mark. Modern editions bracket it.
- q_b204_054, q_b204_055, q_b204_056 (John 8:7–11): the pericope of the adulterous woman. Modern editions bracket it.
- q_b202_020 (Mark 5:1 "Gadarenes"): Luther 2017 has "Gerasener" and RÚF has "gerázaiak". The DE/HU answers follow WEB/Schlachter ("Gadarener", "gadarénusok"). Distractors are clearly wrong either way.
- q_b201_006 (Matthew 15:39 "Magdala"): Luther 2017 has "Magadan". The DE answer uses "Magdala" (as in Schlachter, the verse the app shows).
- q_b201_039 (Matthew 21:1): WEB spells it "Bethsphage". The EN answer copies WEB. The usual spelling is Bethphage (DE Betfage, HU Betfagé).
- q_b203_043 (Luke 6:16 "Judas the son of James"): some Bibles (KJV, Károli?) read "brother of James". Check Károli's wording.
- q_b201_059 (Matthew 23:35 Zachariah son of Barachiah): DE uses "Sacharja" (glossary). Luther 2017 has "Secharja" here.
- q_b203_091 (Luke 17:6 "sycamore tree"): DE "Maulbeerbaum" (Luther), HU "eperfa". Is the HU tree name right for RÚF?
- q_b203_087 (Luke 16:7 "cors of wheat"): DE "Sack" (Luther), HU "véka". The HU unit is unsure.
- q_b203_127, q_b204_043, q_b204_077 (stadia distances): HU "futam" is the Károli unit. RÚF may give kilometres; still recognisable.
- q_b200_036 (Matthew 5:18 "pen stroke"): DE "Tüpfelchen", HU "vesszőcske". Károli/RÚF wording may differ ("pontocska" / "vessző").
- q_b200_084 (Matthew 10:29 "assarion"): DE "Groschen", HU "fillér". These are the Bible-translation conventions, but check them.
- q_b200_099 (Matthew 12:40 "whale"): WEB says "whale". DE answer "des Fisches", HU "cethal".
